Dino Runner

An endless runner for Android, written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. Tap to hop a rock, hold to clear a tall cactus, and keep running from day into night. Everything is drawn in code, with no image assets.

How it's built

  • The game rules are a pure Kotlin Multiplatform module with no Android imports, covered by 60 unit tests.
  • A Compose Canvas draws every frame from an immutable game state, and a ViewModel turns taps and frame ticks into state changes.
  • Jump physics run on real elapsed time, so the arc is the same at 60 or 120 fps.
  • Parallax scenery, the sunset, and the night sky are generated from math, not images.
  • Respects Android's "Remove animations" setting, and TalkBack reads out game over and new high scores.
